# About Paragraphle
Paragraphle is a daily puzzle game based on Wikipedia™. Over 100,000 articles are available as part of the game, a subset that 
was chosen by picking the most linked-to articles within Wikipedia™ itself. Each article is broken into paragraphs and processed 
by an embedding model, which enables the game to compare the semantic similarity between any two chunks of text. This gives you a 
score that lets you know how close your guesses are to the article of the day.
